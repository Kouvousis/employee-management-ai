NovaTech Solutions — Remote Work Policy

NovaTech supports flexible work arrangements, including fully remote, hybrid, and in-office options depending on role and team needs.

Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for remote or hybrid work unless their role has been designated as office-required (currently only facilities and on-site IT roles, none of which exist at NovaTech today).

Core Hours
All employees, regardless of location, are expected to be available and responsive during core hours: 10am–3pm Pacific Time, Monday through Friday. Meetings will generally be scheduled within this window.

Remote Work Setup
NovaTech provides a one-time $1,000 home office stipend to all new remote employees to cover equipment or ergonomic needs beyond the standard laptop provided. Receipts must be submitted through the expense system within 90 days of start date.

In-Person Requirements
Remote employees are asked to travel to their nearest NovaTech office for:
- Company-wide all-hands (twice per year)
- Onboarding week (first week of employment)
- Team offsites (typically once per year per team)

Travel costs for these required in-person events are covered by NovaTech. See the Travel Policy for details.

Security
Remote employees must use a NovaTech-approved VPN when accessing internal systems and must not work from unsecured public networks without VPN active. See the Security Policy for full requirements.

Manager Discretion
Managers may require additional in-office time for specific projects or circumstances with reasonable notice. Discuss any concerns with HR if you feel this policy is being applied unevenly.
