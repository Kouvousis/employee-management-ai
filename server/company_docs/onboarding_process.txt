NovaTech Solutions — Onboarding Process

NovaTech's onboarding program is designed to get new employees productive, connected, and confident within their first 30 days.

Before Day One
HR (Rachel Green or Daniel Torres) will send you a welcome email one week before your start date with instructions for equipment setup, system access requests, and required paperwork. A MacBook Pro and any requested peripherals will arrive before your first day.

Week One — Orientation
All new hires spend their first week at their nearest NovaTech office for in-person onboarding (travel covered by the company for remote employees). The week includes:
- Day 1: Office tour, IT setup, HR paperwork, and a welcome lunch with your team
- Day 2–3: Product and engineering overview sessions led by Leila Nasser and Marcus Chen
- Day 4: Company culture and values session with Sarah O'Brien
- Day 5: Role-specific deep dives with your manager and team

30-60-90 Day Plan
Your manager will share a written 30-60-90 day plan before your start date outlining expectations, key deliverables, and success criteria for your first three months. This plan is a starting point for conversation, not a rigid contract.

Onboarding Buddy
Every new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy — a peer (not your direct manager) who can answer informal questions and help you navigate the company. Buddy assignments are coordinated by Rachel Green.

Tools Access
You will receive access to Slack, Notion, GitHub (for engineering roles), Linear, and Google Workspace on day one. Additional access requests go through your manager.

Check-In Schedule
HR will schedule check-in calls at the 2-week, 30-day, and 90-day marks to collect feedback and address any concerns.
