NovaTech Solutions — 401(k) Plan

NovaTech offers a 401(k) retirement savings plan administered through Fidelity Investments.

Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible to enroll starting on their first day of employment. Enrollment can be completed through the Fidelity NetBenefits portal.

Company Match
NovaTech matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of base salary. The match is deposited each pay period alongside payroll and is immediately vested — there is no cliff or graded vesting schedule.

Contribution Limits
Employees may contribute up to the IRS annual limit ($23,000 for 2025, $30,500 for employees aged 50+). Contributions can be made as traditional pre-tax, Roth after-tax, or a combination.

Investment Options
The plan offers a curated selection of low-cost index funds across equity, fixed income, and target-date categories. A full fund lineup is available in the Fidelity NetBenefits portal.

Auto-Enrollment
New employees are automatically enrolled at a 4% contribution rate (sufficient to capture the full company match) unless they opt out or adjust their contribution rate within 30 days of their start date.

Rollovers
Employees may roll over funds from a previous employer's 401(k) or IRA into the NovaTech plan. Contact Fidelity directly or reach out to David Park (CFO) for assistance.

Loans and Hardship Withdrawals
The plan permits loans and hardship withdrawals under IRS guidelines. These carry tax implications; consult a financial advisor before proceeding.
