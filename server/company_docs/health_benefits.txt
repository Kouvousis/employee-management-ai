NovaTech Solutions — Health Benefits

NovaTech covers 100% of medical, dental, and vision premiums for all full-time employees, and 80% of premiums for dependents. Coverage begins on the first day of the month following your start date.

Medical Insurance
NovaTech partners with Anthem Blue Cross to offer three plan tiers:

- Blue Choice PPO — low deductible, broad network, higher premium (fully covered by NovaTech)
- Blue Choice EPO — mid-tier plan with in-network-only coverage
- High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) — paired with a Health Savings Account (HSA)

Employees who elect the HDHP receive a $1,500 annual HSA contribution from NovaTech in addition to the premium coverage.

Dental Insurance
Delta Dental PPO covers preventive care at 100%, basic restorative care at 80%, and major restorative care at 50% after deductible. Annual maximum benefit is $2,000 per person.

Vision Insurance
VSP Vision covers one eye exam per year, with an allowance of $200 toward frames or contacts annually.

Additional Health Perks
- $75/month wellness reimbursement (gym, fitness apps, meditation subscriptions — see Wellness Benefits)
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP) with free counseling sessions
- Mental health coverage parity — therapy and psychiatric visits treated the same as medical visits under all plans

Open Enrollment
Open enrollment runs each November for benefits effective January 1. Life events (marriage, birth, adoption) allow mid-year changes within 30 days of the event.
