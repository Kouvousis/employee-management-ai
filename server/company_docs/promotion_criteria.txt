NovaTech Solutions — Promotion Criteria

Promotions at NovaTech are based on demonstrated performance at the next level, not tenure or title inflation. The guiding principle is: you should already be doing the job before you get the title.

How Promotions Work
Promotions are initiated by managers during the end-of-year review cycle. A manager who believes a direct report is ready for promotion submits a written case to their VP and to Sarah O'Brien (VP of People). Promotion cases are reviewed in calibration sessions before being approved.

Off-cycle promotions are possible for exceptional circumstances (e.g., a significant expansion of scope) but require VP and CFO approval.

What We Look For
While criteria vary by role and level, promotions generally require demonstrated evidence of:

1. Consistent high performance — not one good quarter, but a sustained track record
2. Operating at the next level — proactively taking on responsibilities beyond current scope
3. Positive team impact — making the people around you more effective
4. Leadership signal — for senior roles, mentoring others and influencing direction
5. Values alignment — embodying NovaTech's values in day-to-day behavior

Level Guides
Each department maintains a career ladder document outlining the competencies and expectations at each level. Ask your manager or Rachel Green for the ladder applicable to your role.

Transparency
Employees are encouraged to have explicit conversations with their managers about promotion readiness. If you believe you are performing at the next level and have not heard a promotion discussed, raise it directly — NovaTech values candid career conversations.

Contact Sarah O'Brien at sarah.obrien@novatech.com with questions about the promotion process.
