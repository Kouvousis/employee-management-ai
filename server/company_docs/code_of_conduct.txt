NovaTech Solutions — Code of Conduct

NovaTech is committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and professional environment for every employee, contractor, and partner. This Code of Conduct sets the baseline expectations for how we treat each other and the people we work with.

Expected Behavior
- Treat every person with respect and dignity, regardless of their role, background, or identity.
- Communicate openly and honestly. Disagreement is healthy; disrespect is not.
- Protect confidential company, client, and employee information.
- Use company resources — time, equipment, budgets — for legitimate business purposes.
- Report concerns, errors, or potential violations promptly rather than covering them up.

Unacceptable Behavior
The following are grounds for disciplinary action up to and including termination:
- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind (see Anti-Harassment Policy)
- Dishonesty, fraud, or misrepresentation
- Unauthorized access to or sharing of confidential data
- Workplace violence or threats
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith

Reporting
Any employee who witnesses or experiences a violation of this Code should report it to Sarah O'Brien (VP of People) at sarah.obrien@novatech.com or, for concerns involving HR leadership, directly to Jane Smith (CEO) at jane.smith@novatech.com. Anonymous reports can be submitted via the company's ethics hotline (details in the employee portal).

All reports are treated confidentially to the extent possible. NovaTech does not tolerate retaliation against employees who report concerns in good faith.

This Code of Conduct applies in all NovaTech offices, at company events, and in any professional context where you are representing NovaTech.
