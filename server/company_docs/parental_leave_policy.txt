NovaTech Solutions — Parental Leave Policy

NovaTech offers generous, equal parental leave to all full-time employees regardless of gender or parental role (birth parent, non-birth parent, or adoptive parent).

Leave Entitlement
- Primary caregiver: 16 weeks of fully paid leave
- Secondary caregiver: 8 weeks of fully paid leave

Employees may designate their own caregiver role. For same-sex couples or situations where roles are unclear, employees self-identify. NovaTech does not require documentation to support the designation.

Eligibility
Employees must have been employed for at least six months before the expected birth or adoption date to receive full paid leave. Employees under six months tenure receive four weeks paid leave and may use PTO for additional time.

Flexibility
Parental leave does not need to be taken consecutively. The 16-week or 8-week allotment can be used in full or split into blocks within the first 12 months following the birth or adoption of a child.

Benefits Continuation
Health, dental, vision, and 401(k) benefits continue uninterrupted during parental leave.

Return to Work
Employees returning from parental leave are guaranteed their same role or an equivalent role at the same compensation level. A phased return schedule (e.g., 3 days/week for the first month) is available by arrangement with your manager and HR.

Planning Your Leave
Notify Sarah O'Brien (sarah.obrien@novatech.com) or Rachel Green (rachel.green@novatech.com) as early as possible — ideally at least 8 weeks before your intended leave start date — so coverage and workload plans can be arranged.
