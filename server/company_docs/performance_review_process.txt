NovaTech Solutions — Performance Review Process

NovaTech runs a structured performance review cycle twice per year: mid-year (June) and end-of-year (December). These reviews inform compensation decisions, promotion discussions, and professional development planning.

Review Components
Each review cycle includes three elements:

1. Self-Assessment
Employees complete a written self-assessment reflecting on their contributions, growth areas, and goals for the next period. The self-assessment form is distributed via the HR portal two weeks before review meetings begin.

2. Manager Review
Managers complete a written evaluation of each direct report covering: impact and results, collaboration and communication, skill development, and alignment with NovaTech's values. Managers are encouraged to gather peer input informally before writing their review.

3. Calibration
Department leads meet with Sarah O'Brien (VP of People) and their respective VP to calibrate ratings across teams before reviews are delivered. This ensures consistency across departments.

Review Meetings
Manager and employee meet 1:1 to discuss the written review. These conversations should be two-way: managers share their assessment, employees respond, and both agree on goals for the next cycle.

Ratings Scale
NovaTech uses a four-level scale:
- Exceeds Expectations
- Meets Expectations
- Partially Meets Expectations
- Does Not Meet Expectations

Compensation Impact
Merit increases and bonuses are determined at the end-of-year review cycle and take effect January 1. Mid-year reviews do not include compensation changes but can inform off-cycle adjustments for significant role changes.
