NovaTech Solutions — Company History

NovaTech Solutions was founded in 2018 by Jane Smith and Marcus Chen, who met while working at a large enterprise software firm in San Francisco. Frustrated by slow-moving organizations that struggled to adopt modern technology, they set out to build a company that could deliver cutting-edge software solutions with the speed and culture of a startup.

The company was incorporated in March 2018 and initially operated out of a co-working space in San Francisco's SoMa district with a team of just four people. The founding vision was simple: help mid-sized businesses modernize their internal tooling and customer-facing platforms.

By the end of 2018, NovaTech had signed its first three enterprise clients and grown to a team of eight. In 2019, the company expanded its leadership team with the addition of Sarah O'Brien as VP of People and David Park as CFO, laying the groundwork for sustainable growth.

In 2020, NovaTech opened its second office in Austin, Texas to tap into the growing tech talent pool in the region. Leila Nasser joined that year as VP of Product, shaping the company's product-led growth strategy.

By 2022, NovaTech had grown to over 20 employees and opened a third office in New York City to support its expanding sales and customer success operations.

Today, NovaTech Solutions employs 25 people across three offices and continues to grow. The company remains privately held and profitable, with a focus on long-term sustainable growth over rapid scaling.
