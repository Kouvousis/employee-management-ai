Cross-Team Collaboration
Engineering and Product work in two-week sprints aligned to quarterly OKRs. Sales and Product hold a bi-weekly sync to communicate customer needs. HR partners with all department leads on headcount planning each quarter.

Headcount decisions above senior individual contributor level require approval from the relevant VP and sign-off from David Park (CFO). New roles are posted internally before being opened externally.
