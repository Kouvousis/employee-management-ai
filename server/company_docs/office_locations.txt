NovaTech Solutions — Office Locations

Headquarters — San Francisco, CA
450 Brannan Street, Suite 300
San Francisco, CA 94107

Our HQ is located in San Francisco's SoMa district, a short walk from Caltrain and multiple BART stations. This office houses Leadership, Engineering, and HR. The space spans two floors with open collaboration areas, private meeting rooms, a fully stocked kitchen, and a rooftop terrace used for team events. The San Francisco office is open Monday through Friday, 8am–7pm.

Austin Office — Austin, TX
701 Brazos Street, Suite 1200
Austin, TX 78701

Opened in 2020, the Austin office is located in downtown Austin and primarily houses the Product and Data & AI teams. The space is designed for deep-focus work with a mix of quiet zones and collaborative pods. It is open Monday through Friday, 8am–6pm CT.

New York Office — New York, NY
1 World Trade Center, Floor 42
New York, NY 10007

Our newest office, opened in 2022, supports our Sales team and serves as the east coast hub for client meetings and partnerships. The New York office is open Monday through Friday, 9am–6pm ET.

Remote Work
A significant portion of NovaTech's team works fully remote or in a hybrid arrangement. Remote employees are expected to overlap with at least one office's core hours (10am–3pm PT) for collaboration. See the Remote Work Policy for full details.

All three offices offer guest Wi-Fi, private phone booths, and standing desks available on request.
