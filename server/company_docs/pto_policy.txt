NovaTech Solutions — PTO Policy

NovaTech offers unlimited paid time off (PTO) to all full-time employees. We believe adults should be trusted to manage their own time, and we do not track vacation days.

How It Works
Employees coordinate time off directly with their manager. There is no approval workflow for requests under two weeks. For absences of two weeks or longer, employees should give at least four weeks' notice and align with their team to ensure coverage.

Minimum Expectations
Unlimited PTO only works if people actually use it. NovaTech expects every employee to take a minimum of 15 days of personal time per year, not including company holidays. Managers are responsible for encouraging their teams to disconnect.

Company Holidays
NovaTech observes 12 paid company holidays per year. See the Holiday Schedule document for the full list.

Sick Leave
Sick days are separate from PTO and are not tracked. If you are unwell, stay home. Extended illness (more than five consecutive days) should be reported to HR so the company can support you appropriately.

Parental Leave
Parental leave is governed by a separate policy. See the Parental Leave Policy document.

End-of-Year Rollover
Because PTO is unlimited, there is no rollover balance and no payout upon departure.
